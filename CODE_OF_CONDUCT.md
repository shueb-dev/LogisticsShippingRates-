# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone who contributes to or participates in this project, regardless of their background, experience, age, nationality, gender, or level of expertise.

We pledge to make participation in this project a positive experience for everyone by encouraging collaboration, respect, constructive feedback, and open communication.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing respect and consideration toward other contributors.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Accepting constructive criticism and feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Being patient and supportive of beginners and experienced contributors alike.
- Respecting different opinions, perspectives, and experiences.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Using offensive, discriminatory, or inappropriate language.
- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Any form of unwelcome or inappropriate behavior that creates an unsafe environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code contributions, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Project maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict access to the project for contributors whose behavior is considered inappropriate, threatening, or harmful to the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Code reviews and project-related communications.
- Community platforms and communication channels associated with the project.
- Public spaces when an individual is officially representing the project or its community.

The Code of Conduct applies equally to all contributors, maintainers, collaborators, and community members.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly and confidentially whenever possible.

Project maintainers are responsible for taking appropriate and reasonable action in response to reported incidents.

Contributors who fail to follow this Code of Conduct may face consequences depending on the severity and frequency of their behavior.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these guidelines when determining the consequences of any violation:

**1. Correction**

For minor violations, contributors may receive a private or public warning explaining the inappropriate behavior and why it was unacceptable.

**2. Warning**

For repeated or more serious violations, contributors may receive a formal warning with clear expectations for future behavior.

**3. Temporary Suspension**

For continued violations or serious misconduct, contributors may be temporarily restricted from participating in project activities.

**4. Permanent Ban**

For severe violations, harassment, threats, or repeated unacceptable behavior, contributors may be permanently banned from participating in the project.

The consequences will be determined based on the severity, context, and impact of the violation.
